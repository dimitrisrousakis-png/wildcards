What are Wildcards?

Wildcards are high-upside companies where the range of long-term outcomes is unusually wide. They are not selected simply because they are fast-growing or fashionable. Each one has a plausible path to becoming materially more valuable if a small number of important technical, commercial or financial assumptions are proven. The model therefore tracks the thesis, evidence, valuation, catalysts and explicit thesis-break conditions rather than reacting to share-price moves alone.

How the Wildcards model works

Thesis states

Improving — fundamental evidence is strengthening. Intact — the original thesis is progressing broadly as expected. Watch — an important assumption is under pressure or still insufficiently proven. Broken — a core thesis-break condition has occurred.

Confidence

Confidence measures how much evidence supports the thesis, not how attractive the share price is. It rises as technical, commercial and financial assumptions are independently validated and falls when uncertainty or execution risk increases.

Valuation states

Attractive means price is at/below the defined entry level. Near Attractive is close to that level. Fair is around probability-weighted value. Extended and Very Extended mean valuation embeds increasingly optimistic outcomes.

Bear / Base / Bull

Each company has three long-term outcome values and probabilities. The PW Fair Value is their probability-weighted value. Scenarios change only when fundamental evidence changes—not simply because the market price moves.

Decision state

The action layer combines thesis health with valuation. A strong thesis can still be Extended, while an Attractive valuation can remain a Watch if the business thesis is under pressure. Broken overrides valuation and becomes Avoid.

Catalyst & thesis break

The Next Catalyst is the evidence needed to advance or validate the thesis. The Thesis-Break Condition defines what would invalidate a core assumption and force a reassessment.

Convexity

Convexity asks whether upside is meaningfully asymmetric relative to downside. Confidence-adjusted convexity tempers that potential by how much of the thesis has actually been demonstrated.

Graduation & bucket

Graduation Stage tracks development from early concept toward commercial scaling. Wildcard Bucket distinguishes emerging, active and scaling opportunities so very different companies are not judged as if they were at the same maturity.
